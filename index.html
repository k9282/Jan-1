Mathematics in Biology / Fig. 1: Leaf-Inspired Curve

This graph uses the polar equation r(θ) = sin(θ) × cos(θ).

The conversion to Cartesian coordinates is: x = r cos(θ) and y = r sin(θ).

The resulting four-lobed mathematical curve has an organic appearance and demonstrates how polar mathematics can generate forms reminiscent of patterns found in nature.

Mathematics in Biology / Fig. 2: DNA Double Helix

This 3D visualization uses two phase-shifted helical curves.

The two strands are connected at regular intervals to represent the geometric idea of DNA base pairs.

The model is a mathematical visualization rather than a molecular-scale biological simulation.

About Jan-1

Jan-1 is an educational web application designed to demonstrate the relationship between mathematical functions, their graphical representation, and corresponding audio tones.

The project also includes biology-inspired mathematical visualizations, including a polar curve and a DNA double helix.

The purpose of Jan-1 is to provide a free and accessible educational tool for students, teachers, researchers, and anyone interested in exploring STEM concepts through interactive visual and auditory representations.
