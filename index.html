Mathematics in Biology / Fig. 1: Leaf-Inspired Curve

This visualization uses the polar equation r(θ) = sin(θ) × cos(θ).

The Cartesian conversion is x = r cos(θ) and y = r sin(θ).

The resulting four-lobed curve demonstrates how polar mathematics can generate organic-looking forms.

Mathematics in Biology / Fig. 2: DNA Double Helix

This 3D visualization uses two phase-shifted helical curves.

The strands are connected at regular intervals to represent the geometric concept of DNA base pairs.

This is a mathematical visualization rather than a molecular-scale biological simulation.

About Jan-1

Jan-1 is an educational web application designed to demonstrate relationships between mathematical functions, graphical representation, and sound.

The project also explores biology-inspired mathematical forms through polar curves and a DNA double-helix visualization.
